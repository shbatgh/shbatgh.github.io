Professional Summary

Sophomore transfer to Carnegie Mellon’s School of Computer Science studying computational biology. Current research in Wei Li’s lab at the University of Maryland Institute for Health Computing covers perturbation-response modeling with optimal transport and flow matching, LoRA adaptation of single-cell foundation models, and benchmarks for paired MERFISH + microscopy data.
